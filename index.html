How to read a connection

A connection means cases share a description of a practice, method, or another selected characteristic. It does not rate the quality of a study. Similar approaches may appear separately when they are described differently.

Selecting a case brings its direct connections closer and moves unrelated cases to the edge. Close the case or choose Show all cases to restore your arrangement. Open Related cases to see what it shares with others. Map positions help arrange the cases; distance is not a measure of similarity.
