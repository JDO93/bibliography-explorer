Why this collection?

This collection grew out of doctoral research on the evaluation of social innovation from a design perspective. The difficulty of finding a bibliography that brought these areas together prompted the creation of a resource to help researchers explore the relevant literature.

The repository brings together case studies, methods, tools, and references in a freely accessible collection. It offers a starting point for researchers seeking to understand connections between evaluation and design, find approaches relevant to their work, and build on existing research.

— Beatriz Bonilla Berrocal

How to read a connection

A line connects two case studies that share at least one recorded value in the dimensions selected under Connect by. A shared method, such as “semi-structured interviews”, can therefore connect studies from different contexts or collections.

To understand a connection, select a case, open Related cases, and choose a study from the list. The comparison shows the shared values alongside the original descriptions, helping you assess what the cases have in common and how their approaches differ.

These links offer starting points for comparison. They depend on the terms used in the collection, so studies that describe similar approaches differently may remain unconnected. A case with no links may still be relevant to your research; use search or Explore values to find it.

Selecting a case brings its direct connections closer to make them easier to explore. Distance on the map does not express similarity, and a connection does not indicate research quality. Choose Show all cases to restore your previous arrangement.
